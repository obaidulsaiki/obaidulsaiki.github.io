# Feature Research

**Domain:** Modern Multi-Page Portfolio
**Researched:** 2026-11-10
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Table Stakes Features

Every top-tier engineering and academic portfolio includes:

| Feature | Standard Implementation | Why Required |
|---------|-------------------------|--------------|
| Responsive Global Navigation | Sticky/Glassmorphism navbar with mobile drawer | Quick access across all 7 routes with active-state indicators |
| Hero & About Section | Profile portrait, crisp title, bio, quick CTAs (Resume, Contact) | Immediate first impression and value proposition within 3 seconds |
| Interactive Skills Matrix | Categorized technology badges with icons (Languages, Frameworks, Cloud/Tools) | Recruiters and collaborators scan skills first |
| Chronological Experience | Role, company, duration, achievements, quantified impact metrics | Proves professional track record and technical depth |
| Education Timeline | Degrees, institutions, honors, key coursework | Core credential verification |
| Publications & Research Showcase | Title, conference/journal, abstract summary, PDF/arXiv/DOI links | Crucial for academic, ML, and research credibility |
| Extracurricular & Leadership | Clubs, mentorship, hackathons, open source initiatives | Demonstrates leadership and collaboration |
| Personal Dimension (Hobbies) | High-personality visual grid, creative interests | Makes candidate memorable and humanizes the profile |
| Frictionless Contact | Direct email copy, social cards (GitHub, LinkedIn, Scholar), quick message | Lowers communication friction for recruiters and peers |

## Differentiating Features (Cool Animations & Polish)

| Feature | Details | User Delight Impact |
|---------|---------|---------------------|
| Route Entrance Choreography | Framer Motion stagger children with subtle fade-up slide | High aesthetic satisfaction during multi-page navigation |
| Floating Skill Badges | Subtle floating physics / hover lift with tooltips | Dynamic interactivity without clutter |
| Interactive Timeline Nodes | Expandable timeline cards with milestone badges | Engaging reading experience for work and education |
| Paper Preview & Citation Copy | One-click BibTeX copy and paper link badges | High utility for researchers and reviewers |
| Dynamic Theme Accent Accents | Harmonious application of `#FFFBF1`, `#FFF2D0`, `#FFB2B2`, `#E36A6A` | Artisanal, non-generic visual identity |

## Feature Mapping by Route

1. **`/` (Home)**:
   - Hero banner with profile picture and punchy career tagline
   - "About Me" career summary narrative
   - Interactive Skills categorized (Frontend, Backend, AI/ML, Cloud/DevOps, Languages) with Lucide & dev icons
   - Direct CTAs to `/research`, `/experience`, and `/contact`
2. **`/education`**:
   - Academic milestones, university credentials, honors, coursework
3. **`/experience`**:
   - Career timeline, positions held, key responsibilities, highlighted wins
4. **`/research`**:
   - Academic publications, preprints, patents, open-source research tools
5. **`/extracurricular`**:
   - Community leadership, student organizations, volunteer work, competitions
6. **`/hobby`**:
   - Creative outlets, reading list, personal projects, visual gallery
7. **`/contact`**:
   - Direct social link buttons, email card with one-click copy, contact inquiry form
